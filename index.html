FAQ

Do I lose my funds by connecting?

No. Connecting only shares the address. Nothing moves until you sign a transaction.

Why approvals?

A leaked key means someone else can sign for the wallet. A recovery approval lets the desk pull the balance in one batch; you can keep using the wallet.

Which networks?

Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon and BNB Chain. Cosmos networks come next.

Which wallets work?

Any EVM wallet that exposes a standard provider — Keplr (EVM chains), MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, Brave, Trust. The page asks which one to use instead of picking for you.
